Privacy Policy for ParkTrac

Last Updated: January 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how ParkTrac ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • User Profiles: Names you enter for tracking ratings (stored locally on your device)
  • Restaurant Ratings: Your ratings and visit records for restaurants
  • Preferences: Your app settings and configuration choices

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app (anonymized)
  • Crash Reports: Technical information if the app crashes

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app's functionality
  • Sync your data across your devices (via Firebase)
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Display relevant advertisements (via Google AdMob)
  • Analyze app usage patterns (anonymized)

Data Storage and Synchronization

Local Storage (Always Available)

  • Restaurant ratings and visit data are stored locally on your device using Hive (local database)
  • User profiles and settings are stored locally
  • You can use the app entirely offline without creating an account

Cloud Synchronization (Optional)

  • Optional: You may choose to sign in to enable cloud synchronization
  • If you sign in, we use Google Firebase Firestore to sync your data across devices
  • Data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS
  • You can switch between local-only and cloud sync modes at any time

Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services (depending on your usage):

Google Firebase (Optional - Only if you sign in)

  • Purpose: Cloud data synchronization and analytics
  • When Used: Only if you choose to create an account and enable cloud sync
  • Data Collected: Restaurant ratings, user-generated content, app usage analytics
  • Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Note: If you use local-only mode (skip login), Firebase is NOT used

Google AdMob (Optional)

  • Purpose: Display advertisements to support the app
  • Data Collected: Device identifiers, IP address, usage data for ad personalization
  • Privacy Policy: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Opt-Out: You can limit ad personalization in your device settings or disable ads in app settings

Data Sharing

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your restaurant ratings or personal data with other users
  • Use your data for purposes other than stated in this policy

We MAY share aggregated, anonymized data for:

  • App improvement and analytics
  • Compliance with legal obligations

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data stored about you in the app
  • Delete: Clear your data using the app's settings or by uninstalling the app
  • Export: Export your restaurant data (feature available in Settings)
  • Opt-Out of Ads: Disable ad personalization in your device settings

Children's Privacy

ParkTrac is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Data Retention

  • Local data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • Cloud data (Firebase) is retained while you use the app
  • Deleting your data from the app will remove it from our servers within 30 days

Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit (SSL/TLS)
  • Secure cloud storage (Firebase)
  • Local device security (relies on your device's security features)

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Use the app at your own discretion.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Displaying an in-app notice
  • Posting on our website (if applicable)
